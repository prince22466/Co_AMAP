Conclusion: the grouped page-lookup specialization is the confirmed source
of the 16K prefill ATTENTION KERNEL slowdown on BI-V150. It is a strong
candidate explanation for the -5.06% end-to-end 16K serving regression;
a separate serving-level ON/OFF experiment is still needed to quantify
how much of the end-to-end difference it explains.
